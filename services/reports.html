FAQ

What documents do I need?
2-3 years of financial statements. Audited is ideal, but management accounts work too. PDF, scanned copies, or Excel — whatever you have.
How many years do you analyse?
Our analysis covers a maximum of 3 financial years or periods. Even if your submitted documents contain additional years of data, the report will focus on the 3 most recent financial years.
How long does it take?
Within 1 working day after we receive your complete documents and payment.
How do I pay?
Starting from RM500 depending on the complexity of your financials — we'll confirm your exact quote before you pay. No surprises.

Who prepares this report?
Built from real banking frameworks — the same credit logic used inside financial institutions — powered by AI for speed and consistency.
